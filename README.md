Project Title:
Market Share Statistical Analysis: Multiple Linear Regression Using R

Project Overview:
Conducted a comprehensive statistical analysis to understand and model the market share of a specific product over a 36-month period. Utilized R programming language to analyze the "Market Share" dataset obtained from the national database (Nielsen). Identified and analyzed factors influencing variations in market share, including average monthly price, Gross Nielsen rating points, discount presence, promotion presence, month, and year.

Key Responsibilities and Accomplishments:
•	Developed and implemented a multiple linear regression model to investigate the relationships between predictor variables and market share.
•	Conducted hypothesis testing to determine the significance of the relationships between independent variables and the response variable.
•	Utilized automatic variable selection methods, criterion-based statistics, residuals diagnostics, and model validation techniques to refine the regression model.
•	Identified and removed non-significant variables (gnrpoints and year) from the model to improve its predictive accuracy.
•	Assessed the proportion of variation explained by each predictor variable using the coefficient of multiple determination (R^2).
•	Investigated partial residual plots, residual-versus-fitted plots, and measures of influence to ensure the validity of the regression model.

Results:
•	Final regression model included the following predictors: price, discount, promotion, and month.
•	Percentage of variation explained by each predictor variable is as follows: price (3.614%), discount (65.066%), promotion (6.47%), month (5.586%).
•	No issues identified with the assumptions of linearity, constant variance, independence, or normality in the residuals.

Tools and Skills:
•	Statistical Analysis: Hypothesis testing, regression analysis, ANOVA.
•	Programming: R programming language.
•	Data Visualization: Partial residual plots, residual-versus-fitted plots.
•	Model Evaluation: Coefficient of determination (R^2), Brown-Forsythe test for homoscedasticity.

Conclusion:
This project demonstrated proficiency in statistical analysis, regression modeling, and programming skills using R. The comprehensive statistical analysis of the market share dataset provided valuable insights into the dynamics influencing the performance of a specific product over a 36-month period. Key predictors such as price, discount, promotion, and month were identified, with discount notably explaining 65.066% of the variation in market share. The final model’s robustness, supported by thorough diagnostics, ensures reliability and accuracy, offering company executives informed guidance on marketing strategies and product development to improve competitive positioning.
